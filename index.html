Background & Philosophy

About Me

Bridging technical empathy with human-centered product thinking.

I am a Junior UI/UX Designer based in Cairo, Egypt. My journey began with a Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science from Thebes Academy, which I completed with an Excellent grade. That engineering foundation taught me how digital systems behave under the hood, how responsive viewports render, and why development constraints matter.

Driven by a desire to craft intuitive interfaces that solve genuine user friction, I pursued formal design education through the UI/UX Design Diploma at Route Academy. There, I immersed myself in the end-to-end design methodology: user research, personas, user journeys, wireframing, interactive prototyping, and usability testing.

Most recently, I completed the Technology Training Program at iCareer Digitera, further strengthening my technical communication, professional workflows, and agile team collaboration.

Why My Computer Science Foundation Benefits Design:

Having a technical background means I design with development feasibility in mind. I structure Figma files with reusable design tokens, auto-layout hierarchies, and clear state variations (default, hover, active, disabled) that make engineer handoffs smooth and implementation accurate.

Competencies

Skills & Design Toolkit

Organized by practical domain capability. Grounded in actual workflow competencies without arbitrary numerical percentage bars.

🧠

UX Design

  • User Research Understanding audience context, identifying pain points, and synthesizing qualitative requirements.
  • Personas & Archetypes Defining behavioral profiles and user mental models to maintain empathy throughout design.
  • User Flows Mapping step-by-step user journeys and decision branches to eliminate friction and dead ends.
  • Information Architecture Structuring content taxonomies, hierarchical sitemaps, and intuitive navigational paths.
  • Wireframing Rapid low-fidelity grayscale sketching in Figma to validate screen ergonomics before styling.
  • Prototyping Building clickable interactive prototypes to test transitions, modals, and flow logic.
  • Usability Testing Conducting heuristic reviews and task-based scenario evaluations to identify usability barriers.
🎨

UI Design

  • Mobile UI Design Designing native-feeling iOS and Android interfaces with thumb-reach ergonomics in mind.
  • Web UI Design Crafting modern, accessible web layouts with structured grids and consistent components.
  • Responsive Design Adapting interfaces smoothly across desktop, tablet, and mobile viewport constraints.
  • Visual Hierarchy Guiding the user's attention through scale, contrast, weight, and intentional whitespace.
  • Typography Establishing readable typographic scales, line heights, and font pairings.
  • Accessibility (WCAG) Ensuring AA/AAA color contrast, minimum 48px tap targets, and legible scalable type.
🛠️
